Privacy Policy

Hide Distracting Items

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Overview

Hide Distracting Items is a Chrome extension that lets you pick elements on a web page (a banner, a sidebar, a pop-up) and hide them. It remembers what you hid for each site and hides it again on later visits. This policy explains what data the extension uses and, just as importantly, what it does not do with it.

No data collection

Hide Distracting Items does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with us or with any third party. There are no analytics scripts, no tracking, no advertising, no remote servers, and no network requests made by this extension at any point. It does not load or run remote code.

What the extension accesses, and why

  • The page you are on — when you click the toolbar icon or use the keyboard shortcut, the extension starts its element picker in that tab. It does not read the text, form fields, or other content of the page. It only works out which element you clicked so that it can hide it.
  • The site address — the extension uses the host name of the site (for example example.com) to look up which items you have hidden there. When a page loads, it re-applies your saved rules. Your browsing history is not recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Screenshots for thumbnails — at the moment you hide an item, the extension takes a screenshot of the visible area of the tab and crops a small thumbnail of just that item, so you can recognise it in the popup and bring it back. The thumbnail is stored only on your device. It may contain whatever was visible in that item at the time.

What's stored, and where

For each site where you hide something, the extension stores the CSS selector of the item, its thumbnail, and the time it was hidden. It also stores the last position of the picker toolbar. This is kept in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. It is not synced to your account and is not accessible to us or anyone else.

You can delete this data at any time: restore an item from the popup, use “Show all hidden items” for a site, or remove the extension, which deletes everything it stored.

Permissions

  • activeTab — temporary access to the current tab after you click the icon or use the shortcut.
  • scripting — to start the picker and to add or remove the style rules that hide your chosen items.
  • storage — to save your hidden items on your device.
  • webNavigation — to re-apply your saved rules as soon as a page or frame loads.
  • Access to http and https sites — so your saved rules can be re-applied automatically on any site where you chose to hide something.

Children's privacy

Hide Distracting Items does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.
